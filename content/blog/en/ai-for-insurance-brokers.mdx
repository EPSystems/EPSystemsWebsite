The broker business in Bulgaria has one recurring problem — **manual work that does not scale**. Comparing policies, preparing quotes, answering customer inquiries, tracking renewals. AI can cut that dramatically.

This guide is for brokers who hear "AI" and wonder "what exactly can it do for my business?" We'll cover 4 concrete use cases with the highest ROI.

## Use case 1: Automatic policy comparison

**The problem:** A customer asks for an auto insurance quote. You need to compare 5-7 offers from different carriers across coverage, exclusions, limits, price. That takes **1-2 hours per inquiry**.

**What AI does:**

You upload the policy terms (PDF or text). AI:

1. Extracts key parameters — coverage, deductible, exclusions, limits
2. Arranges them in a comparison table
3. Flags important differences the customer should know
4. Generates a personalized summary tailored to the customer's profile

Instead of 1-2 hours — **10-15 minutes**. For most brokers that means handling 3x more inquiries.

## Use case 2: AI assistant for customer questions 24/7

**The problem:** The same 15-20 questions every day — when does the policy expire, what does GO cover, how to file a claim, what documents are required. Most arrive after hours.

**What AI does:**

An AI agent trained on your policies, procedures, and common questions runs 24/7 on:

- Messenger
- WhatsApp
- Viber
- Site chat
- Email (automated replies to standard questions)

It answers in Bulgarian, knows your business specifics, recognizes complex cases, and hands them off to you with full context.

**What you get:**

- 60-80% of inquiries handled without you
- Instant reply for the customer instead of 2-24 hour delays
- Zero missed leads outside business hours
- Consistent answers — the same correct thing for the same question

Real example: we built an AI SMS assistant for a Bulgarian insurance broker that handles typical customer inquiries automatically.

## Use case 3: Automated renewal reminders

**The problem:** Manual tracking of expiring policies. The client forgets to renew, goes to a competitor. Direct revenue loss.

**What AI does:**

A system that:

1. Tracks your active client policies (synced with your CRM)
2. 30, 14, and 7 days before expiry sends SMS/email/Messenger reminders
3. Includes a direct renewal link or "I'm interested" reply option
4. Flags non-responders in the CRM for proactive follow-up

Real example: we built SMS reminders for a broker that track expiring policies and send timely notifications. It improves renewal rate without manual tracking.

**What it takes:**
- Integration with your CRM (or Google Sheets if you don't have one)
- SMS/email gateway
- Reminder templates

Typical setup: 1-2 weeks to deploy.

## Use case 4: Automated quote generation

**The problem:** A customer fills out a form on your site. You come back with a quote 1-2 days later. The competitor already replied the same day and took the deal.

**What AI does:**

A form on your site with 5-10 questions → AI:

1. Analyzes the responses
2. Identifies suitable products from your catalog
3. Generates a personalized quote with 2-3 variants
4. Sends it via email + SMS within 30 minutes of submission

The customer gets a quote before they think of a competitor.

**Limits:** AI generates a preliminary quote. Complex cases or ones requiring specific judgment should always be reviewed by a broker before sending.

## Integrations you'll need

For the full stack, typically:

- **CRM** — Excel/Sheets for low volume; Pipedrive, HubSpot, or Bitrix24 for higher
- **SMS gateway** — a local Bulgarian provider
- **Email marketing** — Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, or similar
- **Messenger/WhatsApp Business API** — for chat channels
- **AI layer** — Claude API or equivalent LLM
- **Orchestration** — n8n to tie it all together

Not all of this is needed from day one. We usually start with 1-2 use cases and expand once results come in.
