AI automation is not just for enterprises. For most small businesses in Bulgaria, it can deliver visible impact in **weeks** — not years — if you start from the right place. This guide will show you where.

## What "AI automation" actually means

The term is used loosely, so let's be concrete. "AI automation" is the combination of two things:

- **Automation** — processes that run without human intervention (form → email, new customer → CRM).
- **AI layer** — understands context, reads unstructured data, makes decisions, generates text.

Classic automation: "when an email with `[invoice]` in the subject arrives, save the file to Drive." AI automation: "read the invoice content, extract the amount, date, and supplier, and enter them into the accounting system."

The difference is that AI handles **data that does not follow a fixed template** — which is 80% of real business data.

## 5 processes worth automating first

In our practice with small and mid-sized businesses in Bulgaria, these are the processes with the fastest ROI:

### 1. Answering repetitive customer inquiries

If your team answers the same 10-20 questions every day — pricing, availability, deadlines, procedures — an AI agent with access to your knowledge base can handle 60-80% of them. Customers get an answer in seconds instead of hours.

### 2. Processing incoming documents

Invoices, orders, registration paperwork — AI extracts the key information and enters it into your systems. What used to take 2 hours of manual entry per week takes minutes.

### 3. Tracking and reminders

Expiring policies, upcoming payments, document expiry dates — AI watches the calendar and sends automatic reminders on the right channel (SMS, email, Messenger). Real example from our practice: we built an SMS automation for an insurance broker that tracks expiring policies and sends timely renewal reminders to clients.

### 4. Lead qualification

When someone fills out a form on your site, AI can read what they wrote, score how serious the lead is, and route it by priority. Hot leads get an instant response; the rest go into a nurture sequence.

### 5. Social media and email content generation

LinkedIn post drafts, Instagram captions, onboarding email sequences — AI writes the draft, you review and approve. Time spent on content drops by 70%.

## How to pick the right process

The rule we follow with clients: automate a process that meets **three criteria**:

1. **Repetitive** — happens at least 10 times a week.
2. **Visibly time-consuming** — at least 30 minutes per week in total.
3. **Has clear rules or enough historical data** — there's no point automating something that requires creative judgment every time.

If the process meets all three, ROI is usually under 3 months.

## Where to start this week

The fastest first step you can take **without spending a cent**:

1. Over one week, note every repetitive task you or your team do manually.
2. Group them by type — communication, data processing, tracking.
3. Mark the ones that take the most time or are the most annoying.

That's the input to your AI strategy.
