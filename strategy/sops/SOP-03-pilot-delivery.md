# SOP-03 — Pilot Delivery & Case Study Protocol

Every pilot follows this. The deliverable is not just the automation — it's the **measured number** that becomes the next case study. A pilot delivered without a before/after number is a failed pilot even if the client is happy.

## Day 0 — before any work starts

- [ ] 50% deposit received (not just promised).
- [ ] **Baseline measured and confirmed by the client in writing** (Viber message is fine): which task, who does it, hours per week, error/miss rate if relevant. This sentence is the case study's opening line — without it there is no "after" story.
- [ ] Scope written in one paragraph; client confirms. Case-study clause in the agreement.

## Build (weeks 1–3)

- [ ] Weekly 15-min check-in message to the client (what's done, what's next, what we need from them). Silence kills trust faster than delays.
- [ ] **Scope freeze:** every new idea the client has goes to a "retainer backlog" list, answered with: "Записвам го — това е първото, което правим в месечния план след пилота." New scope never enters the pilot.
- [ ] Case study drafted *during* the build (template below), not after.

## Measure (weeks 3–5)

- [ ] 2 weeks of post-launch data collected the same way the baseline was.
- [ ] Final report to client: baseline vs now, in hours and euros. One page.
- [ ] Remaining 50% invoiced on delivery of the report.

## Harvest (day 30–60)

- [ ] Written testimonial + publication permission. Ask verbatim: "Може ли да публикуваме резултата с ваше име и цитат? Едно-две изречения от вас стигат."
- [ ] Publish case study (site + Emil's LinkedIn), BG first.
- [ ] **Day 45–60: retainer conversation** — "системата работи; за €350–600/месец я поддържаме, следим и добавяме по един процес от списъка ви всеки месец." The backlog list from the build is the sales pitch.
- [ ] **Referral ask regardless of retainer outcome:** three names in their industry, warm intro requested.

## Case study template

> **[Клиент] — [резултат в числа]**
> Проблемът: [1–2 изречения, преди-състоянието с числото от baseline]
> Решението: [какво изградихме, разбираемо за собственик на бизнес]
> Резултатът: [X часа/месец спестени = ~€Y/месец; какво прави екипът с времето]
> „[цитат от собственика]" — [име, фирма]

**Integrity rule (D-004 lesson):** only measured numbers get published. No extrapolations, no "up to", no borrowed results. If the client declines publication, the case study is used anonymized ("счетоводна кантора в София") or not at all.
